# Account Deletion Request & Instructions for ProLive

**App Name:** ProLive  
**Package Name:** `com.liveapp.com`  

In compliance with **Google Play Developer Data Deletion Policies**, **ProLive** provides all users with full control to delete their account and associated personal data at any time.

---

## Method 1: Delete Account Inside the Mobile App (Recommended)

You can delete your account directly inside the **ProLive** app by following these steps:

1. Open the **ProLive** app on your mobile device.
2. Go to your **Profile** tab (bottom right icon).
3. Tap the **Settings** gear icon in the top right corner.
4. Select **Account & Security**.
5. Tap **Delete Account** at the bottom of the screen.
6. Read the deletion warning notice carefully.
7. Confirm your password or authentication method and tap **Permanently Delete Account**.

---

## Method 2: Request Account Deletion via Web or Email (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

1. Send an email to **`support@smartliveapp.com`** from the email address registered with your ProLive account.
2. Set the email subject as: **"Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email / User ID]"**.
3. Include the following details in your email body:
   - Your **User Name** & **User ID** (if known).
   - Your **Registered Email Address** or **Phone Number**.
   - A brief statement: *"I request the permanent deletion of my ProLive account and all associated personal data."*

Our support team will verify your identity and process your deletion request within **5 to 7 business days**.

---

## What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Upon account deletion:
- Your profile credentials, display name, avatar, bio, and social feeds will be permanently removed.
- Your authentication records, direct messages, and chat history will be purged from active databases within **30 days**.
- All unused virtual items, coin balances, diamond rewards, VIP badges, and levels will be forfeited immediately and cannot be recovered.
- Your device ID hardware association with your user account will be unlinked.

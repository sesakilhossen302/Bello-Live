## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users, ranging from basic account setup to interactive real-time audio and video streaming.

### C. Financial & Purchase Data

Payments are processed securely via Google Play In-App Billing or authorized payment gateways; we **do not** collect or store full credit card or bank credentials on our servers.

## 2. Device Permissions Required

To provide live streaming, interactive audio rooms, and social features, **SB Live** requests the following device permissions:

- **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Required to broadcast live video streams, participate in video rooms, and take profile pictures.
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Required for live voice streaming, audio room mic seating, and voice message recording.
- **Photos / Media / Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Required to upload profile photos, share media files in chats, save media, and store app data.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Required to send push notifications for incoming messages, live stream starts, system updates, and reward announcements.
- **Network Access (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Required for online streaming, server communication, and data synchronization.
- **Wakelock (`WAKE_LOCK`):** Keeps the screen active while hosting or watching a live room.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. However, revoking key permissions (e.g., Camera or Microphone) will disable related live streaming features.

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, users have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

### How to Delete Your Account & Data:
1. **In-App Deletion Path:**  
   Go to **Settings** > **Account & Security** > **Delete Account** inside the App and confirm your password/authentication.
2. **Email Request Path:**  
   If you cannot access your account, send an email request to our support team at **`support@smartliveapp.com`** (replace with your support email) from your registered email address with the subject **"Account Deletion Request"**.

### What Happens Upon Deletion:

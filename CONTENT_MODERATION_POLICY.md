# Content Moderation Policy for ProLive

**Effective Date:** July 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 23, 2026  
**Package Name:** `com.liveapp.com`

At **ProLive**, we are committed to maintaining a safe, healthy, and engaging digital space for all users. This **Content Moderation Policy** outlines our systems, rules, workflows, and enforcement procedures for moderating user-generated content (UGC) across live video broadcasts, audio voice rooms, status feeds, comments, and direct messages.

---

## 1. Moderation Framework & System Overview

ProLive uses a **hybrid moderation system** combining artificial intelligence (automated proactive filters) and round-the-clock human moderation.

### A. Automated AI Filtering (Proactive)
- **Text & Keyword Filtering:** Real-time text screen filters detect profane words, hate speech, sexual solicitation terms, contact number sharing, and scam links in live chats and bios.
- **Media & Hash Signature Recognition:** Automated image and video processing algorithms identify explicit nudity, violent imagery, and known CSAM hash signatures before or during broadcasts.

### B. 24/7 Human Moderation Team (Reactive & Proactive)
- **Live Stream Auditing:** Trained human moderators randomly audit active video streams and audio room broadcasts around the clock.
- **Priority Report Queue:** User reports flagged under high-priority categories (e.g., Child Safety, Violence, Nudity) are routed directly to human moderators for immediate review within 1 to 5 minutes.

---

## 2. Moderation Categories & Standards

Content is evaluated against our [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md). Key violation categories include:

| Severity Level | Violation Types | Immediate Moderation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | CSAM/CSAE, Terrorism, Violent Threats, Severe Nudity | Immediate Broadcast Closure + Permanent Account Ban + NCMEC/Police Report |
| **High** | Sexual Suggestiveness, Harassment, Hate Speech, Scams | Broadcast Closure + Mic Mute + 24hr to 7-day Account Suspension |
| **Medium** | Inappropriate Attire, Copyrighted Music/Video, Spamming | Broadcast Warning + Mic Mute / Temporary Feature Restriction |
| **Low** | Off-topic Spam, Minor Room Disturbance | In-app Warning / Room Kick by Host |

---

## 3. Room Host & Co-Host Moderation Rights

To foster community-led safety, room hosts and designated co-hosts possess localized moderation tools:
- **Mic Mute:** Temporarily or permanently mute any mic seat occupant in an audio/video room.
- **Kick Out:** Remove disruptive viewers or guests from the room.
- **Room Blacklist:** Prevent specific users from re-entering the host’s live room.

---

## 4. Reporting & Resolution Workflow

1. **User Submits Report:** Users tap the **Report Flag Icon** on a stream, profile, or message.
2. **System Categorization:** The report is prioritized by AI based on severity.
3. **Moderator Review:** A safety team member views live stream snapshots, chat logs, or media uploads.
4. **Action Taken:** If a violation is confirmed, appropriate penalties (warning, stream closure, suspension, ban) are executed immediately.
5. **Reporter Notification:** The reporting user receives an in-app notification confirming action was taken.

---

## 5. Appeals & Misaction Review

Users who believe their stream was closed or account was penalized in error can submit an appeal:
- **Email:** `support@smartliveapp.com`
- **Subject:** "Moderation Appeal - [User ID]"
- Appeals are reviewed by a senior safety supervisor within **48 hours**.

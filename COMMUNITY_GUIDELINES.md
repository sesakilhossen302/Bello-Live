# Community Guidelines for ProLive

**Effective Date:** July 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 23, 2026  
**Package Name:** `com.liveapp.com`

Welcome to **ProLive**! Our mission is to provide a vibrant, fun, safe, and respectful environment where users around the world can connect, host live video broadcasts, enjoy audio voice rooms, play mini-games, and build positive communities.

These **Community Guidelines** apply to all users, hosts, agencies, and viewers on **ProLive**. By accessing or using the App, you agree to comply with these rules. We enforce a **ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY** for objectionable content, illegal activities, and abusive behavior.

---

## 1. Core Principles

- **Be Respectful:** Treat fellow hosts, mic seat occupants, and viewers with dignity and kindness.
- **Keep it Safe:** Never share or encourage dangerous, illegal, or harmful actions.
- **Respect Privacy:** Protect your personal safety and do not expose private information of others without explicit consent.
- **Maintain Authenticity:** Do not engage in impersonation, fraud, spam, or misleading behavior.

---

## 2. Zero-Tolerance & Prohibited Content

The following types of content and behaviors are strictly forbidden across all areas of **Bello Live** (including live streams, voice rooms, profile pictures, bios, status feeds, direct chats, and comments):

### A. Sexual Content & Nudity
- Full or partial nudity, explicit sexual acts, or pornography.
- Erotic or sexually suggestive audio broadcasting or erotic performances.
- Solicitation of sexual services or non-consensual sexual harassment.
- **Child Sexual Exploitation (CSAM):** Absolutely forbidden. Any depiction or solicitation involving minors will result in immediate permanent ban, device block, and reporting to legal authorities (including NCMEC).

### B. Harassment, Bullying & Hate Speech
- Harassing, stalking, threatening, insulting, or intimidating any user or host.
- Promoting discrimination, violence, or hatred based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
- Encouraging mass-reporting or targeted online attacks against individuals.

### C. Dangerous & Harmful Behavior
- Depicting, promoting, or encouraging self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders.
- Broadcasting dangerous stunts, drug use, heavy alcohol abuse, or illegal weapon usage.
- Promoting violent extremism, terrorism, or dangerous organizations.

### D. Fraud, Scams & Financial Exploitation
- Soliciting money, bank details, or passwords under false pretenses.
- Buying, selling, or trading virtual coins/diamonds outside official Bello Live platforms or authorized portals.
- Running unauthorized lotteries, pyramid schemes, or illegal gambling outside integrated in-app mini-games.

### E. Impersonation & Misinformation
- Posing as Bello Live official staff, moderators, celebrities, or other users to deceive others.
- Spreading malicious rumors, misinformation, or defamatory content.

### F. Intellectual Property & Copyright Violations
- Re-broadcasting copyrighted movies, TV shows, live sports events, or copyrighted music without proper authorization.
- Using trademarked logos or assets without permission in profile assets or promotional materials.

---

## 3. Live Video & Voice Room Specific Rules

### For Hosts & Co-Hosts:
- **Room Moderation:** Hosts are responsible for keeping their live streams and voice rooms orderly. Hosts must kick or mute mic seaters who violate Community Guidelines.
- **Active Streaming:** Streaming inactive/blank screens, static images for extended periods, or sleeping on camera to farm virtual rewards is prohibited.
- **Decent Attire:** Hosts must wear appropriate clothing suitable for a public broadcast.

### For Mic Seat Occupants & Viewers:
- Respect the room host and other mic guests.
- Do not play excessively loud, offensive, or copyrighted sound effects/music that disrupt the room.
- Do not spam the live chat with repetitive text, promotional links, or gift-begging messages.

---

## 4. User Safety & In-App Reporting Tools

Bello Live empowers users to protect themselves and maintain community quality through the following safety tools:

- **Block User:** You can block any user at any time. Blocked users cannot message you, enter your private rooms, or view your feed.
- **Report Content / User:** Tap the **Report** flag icon on any stream, profile, chat message, or post to alert our 24/7 Safety & Moderation Team.
- **Host Room Controls:** Room hosts can mute, kick, or ban specific users from entering their live room.

---

## 5. Violation Enforcement & Penalties

Our automated moderation algorithms and 24/7 human moderation team review reported content continuously. Violations will result in actions depending on severity:

1. **Warning Notice:** In-app warning for minor or unintentional violations.
2. **Feature Restriction:** Temporary mute from live room chat, temporary loss of mic seat privileges, or status post lock.
3. **Stream Termination:** Immediate force-closing of live video streams or audio rooms.
4. **Temporary Account Suspension:** 24-hour, 7-day, or 30-day account lockout.
5. **Permanent Account Ban:** Permanent deletion of account credentials, forfeiture of remaining virtual coin balances, and device ID hardware block.
6. **Law Enforcement Escalation:** Severe offenses (e.g., child exploitation, terror threats, financial fraud) will be reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

---

## 6. How to Appeal a Penalty

If you believe your account was suspended or your live stream was terminated in error, you may submit an appeal:

- **Email Support:** Send an email to **`support@smartliveapp.com`** with the subject **"Account Appeal - [User ID]"**.
- **Include:** Your User ID, registered email/phone, a detailed description of the incident, and any supporting evidence or context.

Our Safety Team reviews all appeals within **48–72 hours**.
